# NOUX · pre-departure UX audit (21 November 2026)

This review covers the publicly versioned application layers and release workflows, not plaintext modules inside the private encrypted vault. No itinerary changes or new bookings are assumed.

## Delivered: Food guide

- 13 Food Wish List entries now have branch-specific addresses, standard published opening hours, source sites, Thanksgiving exceptions, suggested geographic days, and direct directions / current-hours lookup links.
- The new Food guide renders outside the React root. It is reachable from the Wishlist's contextual button, clicking a recognised Food card, or Explore.
- The original four-element private Food Wish rows, priorities and itinerary decisions remain unchanged.
- An unmatched personally added Food entry is clearly labelled as having unverified location and hours, with a search link rather than invented data.
- Holiday warnings are explicit. In particular Russ & Daughters and Lobster Place are closed on 26 Nov; Katz's publishes an 18:00 Thanksgiving closing. Standard hours for Levain and Magnolia differ between official listings.

## Pragmatic product improvements to consider

| Priority | Improvement | Practical benefit | Constraint / release safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Offline Essentials | Display the encrypted day plan and critical addresses when mobile reception fails | The current repo has a PWA manifest but no registered service worker; only encrypted vault bytes may be cached, not decrypted itinerary or keys |
| P0 | Booking Readiness | One screen lists confirmed events, tickets still required, exact cruise pier, theatre admission, return coach and timed museum entries | Never imply that a selected option equals a booked ticket; preserve booked events |
| P1 | GO MODE | A single mobile next-stop view with leave-by time, route, walking/subway transfer and correct platform direction | Reuse existing MTA and route components, distinguish live predictions from planned timetable |
| P1 | Near Me Food | Suggest a quick bite near the current stop, with a live Google Maps hours check and one-tap directions | Browser location must be optional; no fabricated open-now information or automatic detours |
| P1 | Rain / Wind fallback | Offer an indoor substitute within the same neighbourhood when rooftop, sunrise or waterfront plans become impractical | Preserve ticketed events and user choices |
| P2 | Spending and keepsakes | Approximate tax/tip and per-day spending totals, optional trip photos | Keep personal information locally encrypted and small enough for offline use |
| P2 | Seamless shared itinerary refresh | Apply the other device's decision without relocking or refreshing the page | Current safe reload approach remains the fallback until React state rehydration is fully regression-tested |

## Release discipline

Do not touch the encrypted private app or add DOM children within React-managed navigation. Initialise new modules after the healthy first paint; maintain mobile layout tests, local data preservation and two-device sync tests. Prioritise dependable offline access and booking readiness ahead of cosmetic features.
